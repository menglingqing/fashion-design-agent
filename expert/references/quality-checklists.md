## 1. Universal checks

- Does every conclusion distinguish evidence, inference, and recommendation?
- Are source, date, market, channel, and metric definitions recorded for current data?
- Are assumptions and unknowns visible?
- Do category plan, style brief, visuals, copy, and timeline agree?
- Is the next approval gate explicit?
- Are confidential brand data and supplied references excluded from shareable output unless authorized?

## 2. Seasonal plan

- Strategy: Does each major category answer a brand and consumer opportunity?
- Range: Do GSV/mix/SPU/gender/price/launch totals reconcile?
- Roles: Is every SPU assigned a commercial role?
- Story: Does the theme affect product, material, color, and details rather than only mood imagery?
- Scenario: Is each scenario connected to action, climate, pain point, and category?
- Benchmark: Are business, design, material, and communication references separated?
- Materials: Are function requirements supported by evidence or marked pending?
- Timing: Are decision, sample, order, shoot, warehouse, and launch milestones feasible and owned?
- Risk: Are big bets, supplier constraints, and fallback options visible?

## 3. Reference analysis and design extension

- Is the reference's category, silhouette, proportion, material behavior, construction, and style language described accurately?
- Are invisible details labeled uncertain rather than guessed?
- Is `retain / transform / avoid` documented?
- Does each direction change at least three visible design dimensions?
- Are logo, artwork, proprietary hardware, and distinctive panel combinations avoided?
- Can the final direction be recognized as a new design at first glance?

## 4. Rendering and technical flats

- Is the intended output identified as rendering, flat, detail sheet, colorway, or presentation board?
- Are front and back views consistent in length, seams, closures, pockets, and trim placement?
- Are left/right orientation and asymmetry correct?
- Does the fabric drape match its claimed weight and structure?
- Are proportions plausible on the target wearer?
- Are technical flats orthographic, neutral, unshaded or minimally shaded, and free of perspective distortion?
- Are callouts legible and linked to the correct feature?
- Are typography, grid, margins, and labeling consistent across boards?
- Is there any clipping, overlap, garbled text, or accidental extra garment detail?

## 5. Product brief and tech pack

- Are wearer, scene, price, role, target, and launch defined?
- Are silhouette, ease, length, and key proportions explicit?
- Are all construction details visible in a drawing or written callout?
- Do drawing callouts, BOM, measurements, and colorways match?
- Are composition, weight, finish, color standard, and test status recorded where applicable?
- Are seam, stitch, edge finish, closure, reinforcement, labels, artwork, and packaging covered?
- Are tolerances, grade rules, and fit model data present or clearly TBD?
- Does every TBD have an owner and due date?
- Is version/status visible on every released document?

## 6. Sample review

- Is the sample version and measurement condition recorded?
- Are comments based on observable evidence or measurements?
- Are critical function and safety issues reviewed before aesthetic refinements?
- Are fit balance, mobility, layering, and target posture considered?
- Does each requested change state location, amount/target, and verification method?
- Are linked changes considered, such as sleeve cap versus armhole or pocket move versus internal construction?
- Is approval status unambiguous?

## 7. Selling points and claims

- Does every selling point connect a feature to a wearer benefit and scene?
- Is an “upgrade” compared with a named prior state?
- Is an “innovation” genuinely new and evidenced?
- Are numeric or regulated claims supported by current test reports?
- Are “waterproof,” “sun protection,” “antibacterial,” “sustainable,” “temperature rise,” and similar claims verified?
- Does the copy match the final approved garment rather than an early concept image?
- Are unverified claims removed or rewritten as design intent?
